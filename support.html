Frequently asked questions
Everything you might want to know about Voyeza.
How do I delete my account?▾

Go to Settings → Account & Privacy → tap "Delete Account". This permanently deletes all your posts, passport stamps, messages, and profile data. This action cannot be undone.

Why can't I see someone's profile?▾

The account may be private (only approved followers can see their content), the account may have been deleted, or you may have been blocked by that user. If you believe this is an error, contact us.

How do passport stamps work?▾

When you post a photo or Trailshot with a location tagged, Voyeza automatically adds a stamp for that country to your passport. Visits to the same country within about 30 days are grouped into one trip. You can filter your passport by year to see your travel history.

What are Trailshots?▾

Trailshots are 24-hour photos or videos that disappear after a day unless you save them to your passport. They appear in the trail strip at the top of the feed and are designed for sharing real, in-the-moment travel content.

Can I upload old travel photos?▾

Yes. When creating a post, tap the date field to change it to when the photo was actually taken. Your passport and travel stats will reflect when you actually visited, not when you uploaded.

How do I report harmful content?▾

Tap the three-dot menu (···) on any post, comment, or profile and select "Report". Choose the reason and submit. We review all reports and respond within 24 hours for serious safety matters. For urgent issues email voyeza.app@gmail.com.

Is Voyeza free?▾

Yes, Voyeza is completely free to download and use. We don't currently show ads. In the future we may introduce optional premium features, but the core app will remain free.

What is the minimum age for Voyeza?▾

You must be 16 or older to use Voyeza. This is a legal requirement under the Australian Online Safety Amendment (Social Media Minimum Age) Act 2024. Accounts found to belong to users under 16 will be immediately terminated.

Where is my data stored?▾

Your account data, posts and metadata are stored on Google Firebase servers in Iowa, United States (us-central1 region). Your photos and videos are stored separately on Cloudflare R2 object storage in the Oceania region. Both providers implement internationally recognised security standards. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

I'm in the EU — what are my rights?▾

EEA users have full GDPR rights including data access, portability, restriction, objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Contact us at voyeza.app@gmail.com to exercise any of these rights.

I'm in California — what are my rights?▾

California users have CCPA rights including the right to know what data we collect, the right to deletion, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Contact us at voyeza.app@gmail.com.

Online safety

Your safety matters. Voyeza complies with the Australian Online Safety Act 2021 and the requirements of the eSafety Commissioner. If you've experienced cyberbullying, image-based abuse, or harmful content, you can report it to us or go directly to the eSafety Commissioner.
